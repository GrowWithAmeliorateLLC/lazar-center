Why teach at Lazar Center

A different kind of teaching experience.

Lazar was founded by an educator with a simple belief: every student can succeed when instruction is personalized to the way they learn. That’s why we’ve created a culture built on collaboration, high standards, and meaningful support, giving educators the freedom to focus on what matters most: helping students grow.

A work schedule that fits your life.

We are open Monday through Thursday from 3:00 to 8:00 p.m., with optional Saturday hours from 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. We’re looking for educators who can commit to a consistent schedule so students benefit from working with the same tutor every session.

Individualized instruction that works.

Our 2:1 student-to-tutor ratio works better for students and teachers. You will have the time to ask questions, adjust your instruction, celebrate breakthroughs, and help students master concepts instead of simply moving on. It’s teaching the way most educators wish they could teach every day.

Built by educators. For educators.

At Lazar, you will work alongside experienced directors and fellow educators who collaborate, share ideas, and support one another every step of the way. We provide the curriculum and coaching while managing scheduling, billing, and family communication so that you can focus on what matters most: inspiring students and changing lives.

Open positions

Join one of our three locations.

We’re always looking for passionate educators to join our teams in Charlotte (Myers Park), Tega Cay, and Fort Mill. If you’re excited about helping students reach their full potential, we’d love to hear from you. Complete the application below, and we’ll be in touch if there’s a great fit.

Educators

Teachers, tutors, and passionate educators who believe every student deserves personalized instruction. Whether your expertise is elementary reading, middle school math, high school coursework, or SAT/ACT prep, you’ll have the opportunity to help students build confidence, master new skills, and achieve meaningful results.

Advanced STEM Specialists

Professionals and advanced degree holders with deep expertise in advanced mathematics and science. If you’re passionate about sharing your knowledge in AP courses, gifted education, or SAT/ACT prep, you’ll work with motivated students who are eager to be challenged.

Operations & Admin

Our Operations & Administrative team keeps every Lazar center running smoothly. These team members create an exceptional experience for families, coordinate schedules, and support our educators so they can focus on changing students’ lives. While these positions are less common, we’re always excited to connect with outstanding candidates.
