What a summer plan looks like

8 weeks · 7th grade math

Weeks 1–2 · Core Foundations
Integer operations, fraction operations, order of operations
Rebuild accuracy and confidence with foundational skills
Weeks 3–4 · Review
Unit rates, proportional reasoning, solving proportions
Apply concepts to real-world problem solving
Weeks 5–6 · Strengthen
Simplifying expressions, one-step and two-step equations
Build fluency and step-by-step problem-solving skills
Weeks 7–8 · Get Ahead
Intro to linear equations, variables, and graphing
Prepare for key concepts in the upcoming school year
Why summer matters

Summer learning
that lasts.

Consistent, targeted instruction over the summer is one of the most effective ways to maintain and accelerate progress.

Stay consistent.

Students work with the same dedicated tutor throughout the summer, building trust, momentum, and the consistency that leads to real progress. No resets, just steady growth.

Stay informed.

After every session, parents receive clear, detailed updates on what was covered, where progress is happening, and what comes next. You always know how your student is doing.

Work ahead.

We reinforce key concepts from the previous school year while introducing what's coming next, so students walk into the new school year confident, prepared, and ahead.

How summer at Lazar works

Built around your child,
not a curriculum.

Summer is one of the most valuable times for your child to make meaningful academic progress. With fewer school-year pressures, we can focus on what actually matters: building confidence, strengthening key skills, and preparing for a strong start.

Personalized plans, not summer camps.

Every plan is tailored to your child's specific needs and goals. We identify gaps, prioritize what matters most for the year ahead, and build instruction around the student in front of us, not a generic curriculum.

Consistent weekly sessions.

The same tutor, every session. Students build relationships, momentum, and accountability. Progress is stronger when summer learning feels consistent, not occasional.

Flexible scheduling.

Summer is busy. Camps, vacations, family time. We work around your family's calendar to find a rhythm that fits, with sessions that flex around what's already on the schedule.

Built around what comes next.

We use the summer to strengthen what matters and get a head start on what's next. With fewer academic pressures, students build real understanding and confidence that carries into the fall.
