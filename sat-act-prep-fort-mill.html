How SAT and ACT prep works at Lazar Fort Mill

We start with where your student is right now, using prior scores, school performance and targeted assessments when needed. From there, every lesson, assignment and pacing plan is built for your student and their test of choice. There's no one-size-fits-all curriculum.

Strategy is part of every session: timing and pacing, question patterns, and decision-making under pressure. Graded practice tests show progress along the way, and you'll know what was covered after each session.

Questions parents ask

SAT or ACT: which one should my student take?

We prep for both. In the free consult we look at your student's prior scores, school performance and goals, and recommend the test that fits them best.

How many hours of prep does my student need?

Programs run 24 to 36 hours of instruction. Most families choose 30 to 36 hours to give strategies time to take hold before test day.

How many students work with each tutor?

Test prep keeps a 2:1 student-to-tutor ratio, so your student gets personal attention and real interaction with an experienced educator.

Are practice tests included?

Scholar includes 4 full-length, graded practice tests and Premier includes 6. Each one shows exactly where the next points will come from.

How do we get started?

Book a free 15-minute consult. A Lazar director talks through your student's goals and timeline, then recommends a program.
